### Interim 1
Having returned from his first Delta Green field mission, finally coming face to face with the unnatural (more so than the plane crash), Lyle is in a focused mood. It is strange to have seen the truly strange, but Lyle knows there is more to come and the road ahead may be difficult. In the back of Lyle's mind, he wishes to spend some quality time with Harper and Garrick, as it is very valuable to him. However, Lyle's immediate focus is all on the unnatural events of his own experience, and potentially Garrick's.

Lyle has a warm welcome and a brief respite, which quickly turns into a late night of discussion with Garrick. Lyle doesn't divulge what happened, but Garrick has told enough of his own ghost stories from his sailing days to recognize his mood, so they spend the night talking of the strange and occult.

The next day, Lyle is eager to see what is on those FBI evidence tapes from the cabin. He wants a good idea of what information they contain before he hands them over to his superiors in the FBI. Lyle spends a good chunk of his free time each day researching and investigating anything related to the tapes and the things he's experienced.

After some time, when Lyle has satisfied enough of his curiosity and leads, he puts his occult investigations on hold to spend time with Garrick and Harper. It's a breath of fresh air for Lyle to get out on the water, fishing with Garrick, water-skiing with Harper, hanging out around campfires, and other such activities. Lyle is also sure to practice his shooting, of course, he's gotta stay sharper than ever.

### First Delta Green Field Mission (October, 2015)
Lyle's first flight since the crash feels very long, as he stomachs a cold sweat, constantly vigilant for anything weird. He does his best to stay calm and collected, with some success, reciting stoic/Vulcan-like mantras to himself.

> "Pain is a thing of the mind. The mind can be controlled." — Spock

Lyle felt quite good to get off the plane and is the first to arrive at the meeting place. He is met by the handler, and eventually: McMurty, Professor of Anthropology, Seymour, and Special Agent Ivan. The handler details the situation:
- A Delta Green agent was found dead in his apartment
- We four are tasked with
	- Investigation of the situation
	- Cover up of the situation
	- All before the agent's relatives show up (about 24 hours)

Taking the keys for the apartment and leaving the handler's phone for Seymour, Lyle pairs up with McMurty and has an oddly nice time driving over while listening to the Beastie Boys. Seymour and Ivan wait in their car while Lyle awkwardly sneaks to the door and McMurty deftly glides in. They don't find much at first, but digging deeper reveals some unimportant details of the agent's death, and more interesting details on a cabin.

They discover that this cabin has something hidden underground and the agent posthumously requests that we simply burn it and sweep away the ashes. Seymour and Ivan seem more in favor of following his directions, but Lyle and McMurty figure they ought to gather more information, especially considering they can't necessarily trust this agent. Relevant items found included gas cans meant for burning the thing underground and a box with artifacts that would interest Delta Green, along with FBI evidence tapes, which Lyle would later claim for his own research.

The thing in the septic tank was discovered by Seymour peeing into it. It did it's best to convince us of its innocence, and it had some success, as we decided to not act rashly based on what little info we had. Luckily, we were ready to handle the situation as it eventually dropped its veil of fakery. We shot it and slashed it with decent success, finally knocking it out. Lyle wasn't going to trust that it was dead and having already called for someone to get the gas to burn it (which Ivan did), they threw it back into its prison.

It was difficult to keep it contained, but Lyle blasted it face in with a great shot and we were all able to seal it in and burn it. The horrid shrieks of its unnaturally long burning time were chilling, but it eventually subsided.

The group made one mistake trying to leave in the muddy rainstorm, as they got a car stuck and Delta Green had to deal with it for us. Other than that, Lyle felt pretty good about his first Delta Green field mission.

### Before 2015
Lyle grew up in Vermont, living most of his life without his father. Lyle isn't quite sure what happened to his father. Thankfully, Lyle found a father figure in Garrick Thorne. Lyle learned many things from Garrick, including: fishing, tying knots, boating, and more. Lyle still lives in Vermont, staying close to his mom, Harper, and his closest companion, Garrick.

Lyle has had various physical jobs and a few with a more intellectual focus. He's been out on boats, in work crews, and managing different groups. Lyle aims to be a jack of all trades but he does gravitate toward active things, as he always enjoys shooting, fishing, boating (from large sailing boats to canoes), and casual sporting. Lyle makes enough money to sustain himself (not that it takes too much) and to split a good portion with his mother and Garrick.

In more recent times, Lyle has been focused more on his FBI Special Agent career and his involvement with Delta Green. These pursuits have provided a clearer set of goals for Lyle. He is motivated by physically protecting people, and now that he knows more about how strange and disturbing the universe can be, he's more determined than ever to hone himself. When Lyle stares into the abyss, he avoids its gaze if he can help it, but he keeps his own eyes trained on it, fighting through whatever fear may come of it.

As Lyle learns more about how small and insignificant humanity is in the face of the unfaceable, he sees it as an oppression. If Lyle can at least use his small and insignificant will to fight against that oppression, he will have achieved happiness. Lyle asks himself, "do these things even know happiness as we do?" For all of their incomprehensibility, maybe they do, or maybe they don't. Either way, they are the oppressors.

Lyle had a frightful introduction to the unnatural during a commercial flight in 2011. He saw something that shouldn't exist, which caused the plane to crash. (Probably something similar to the Twilight Zone gremlin on the wing.) Luckily, he survived this crash along with a handful of others. Lyle was always entertained by Garrick's ghost stories from his sailing days, but after his own encounter, he listens more intently than ever. Lyle confided in Garrick with his own story and spent the rest of the day discussing the oddities of life while fishing.

The next flight Lyle would take was his flight to his first field mission for [[Delta Green]]. Lyle has been on plenty of ordinary field missions for the FBI. Maybe even encountering odd things people usually don't see, but now Lyle knows there are weirder things out there. Lyle is at least glad to have spent much of his life shooting guns (at least small arms). Lyle has also started to learn about computers since he started working for the FBI, as they are very important these days.

Lyle hasn't made many enemies, other than perhaps those who eventually clash with one from their differing trajectories. He also hasn't garnered many friendships beyond casual work acquaintances; although, the fierce conditions of being a Special Agent and handling Delta Green could expand his circle. Those he is most likely to call friends are those who consistently stick with him, pursuing similar goals, which is bound to be his field partners in the FBI and Delta Green.

Many of Lyle's best childhood memories involve Garrick and/or Harper. From the many deep and light conversations during fishing to the nostalgic warmth of Harper's presence.
